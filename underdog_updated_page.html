The process

Four steps from first call to a site you run yourself.

At Underdog, we use a site-building approach that caters to small businesses and orgs. We design and build on a live site, circumventing the mockup-and-wait cycle: you’re seeing your actual site from the first week.

01

Talk it through

A short call about your work, your audience, and the sites you like. We gather your photos, copy, and any branding you already have.

~1 hour

02

Design it live

We build a working draft with options built in: layouts, colors, text length, small details. You flip between them on the real site and pick what feels right.

Choices, not guesses

03

Lock it in & launch

Once you've chosen, the options come out and the final site goes live on fast, reliable Vercel hosting at your own domain.

Your domain, fast hosting

04

Hand it over

You get your own AI-powered edit portal. Ask for a change in plain words, review a preview, approve it. We're a message away for bigger work.

Changes in minutes
